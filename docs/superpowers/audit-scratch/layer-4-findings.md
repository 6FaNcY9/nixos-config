# Layer 4 Findings: nixos-modules/

## Grep results summary

`grep -r "bandit\|vino"` found two hits, both in `core/nix.nix` (lines 35, 40): the
personal Cachix cache URL and its public key. Neither references a hostname or username
variable — they are literal strings that identify a cache specific to this owner.
